SYSTEM NOTES

The engine behind the map

Every dot on the map is real. Nothing is scraped, scheduled, or generated to fill space. The map is fed by one MCP server — brain-mcp — and one file of truth, brain.json. Agents can only propose; mass is earned, never granted by the machine.

01 · CAPTURE AT THE POINT OF THOUGHT

There are no sources to scrape because the place the thinking happens — Claude Code sessions — is the capture surface itself. Mid-work, when a claim crystallizes, I say “particle that” and the session agent calls brain_propose. The cost of capture is saying yes.

A nightly cron is the backstop: decay runs, a cartographer agent reads the ledger and may chart at most two suggestions, and the file is committed — the site rebuilds. Promotion to a full orbiting note only ever happens by human approval, in chat.

02 · THE FIVE TOOLS

brain_propose
any session agent · appends a particle, max 6 per day
brain_promote
human approval only · particle becomes an orbiting note
brain_remember
human only · a memory: whole, undecaying, inside the horizon
brain_chart
nightly cartographer · at most 2 cited suggestions in deep space
brain_state
anyone, read-only · ids and titles for reference and dedupe

Decay is deliberately not a tool. Entropy is not optional — it runs from cron: energy cools 8% nightly, unengaged suggestions expire after 14 days, and note ages are recomputed so orbits stay honest.

03 · GUARDRAILS — THERMODYNAMIC, NOT BUREAUCRATIC

Rate. Six proposals a day. Scarcity forces editorial judgment upstream.
Substance. Titles that are pure activity (“worked on X”) are rejected. Claims only, never diary.
Dedupe. Strong title overlap merges — energy adds, references union — instead of appending.
Quarantine. Uncertain affinity stays null. Agents may not invent themes; those are the gray drifters.
Accretion. Three mutually similar dark particles surface as a cluster; only a human names the new theme.
Provenance. Every particle keeps its source and date forever.

05 · LOOK AT IT

The whole thing is small on purpose: a stdio MCP server, one dependency-free logic module where the guardrails live, a test file that is the contract, and a shell script cron calls at 23:50.
