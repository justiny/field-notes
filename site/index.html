JUSTIN YOUNG · FIELD NOTES
A map of what I’m thinking about, drawn as gravity

Nothing here is backfilled. Every dot was captured from a real working session, and the map only gains mass when I say so. Fly around it — it is the whole thing, and there is no other page.

THEMES
The green forms on the ring. Each crystallizes one more side per note it holds, and its mass raises a tide on the membrane.
NOTES
Orbit the theme that produced them — close and fast when new, drifting wider and dimmer with age. Click one to read it.
PARTICLES
Raw claims, not yet promoted. Green ones are falling toward a theme; gray ones are unclaimed and may cool and drift out.
MEMORIES
Inside the black horizon at the centre. Dive in to find them — they ignore time and they are not leaving.
UNCHARTED
Orange, out past the particles. Territory the nightly cartographer reasoned its way toward from what is already here, and that I have not visited yet. Click one to read the case for going.
STARS
Everything I haven’t thought about yet. Click one to take a sighting off it and find out where this place actually is.
TIMELINE
Drag the scrubber along the bottom to rewind and watch the map assemble itself from day one.
